# Claude Guidance for the CStar Archive

CStar is inactive legacy source. Follow the parent Corvus Organism projection
and this repository's `AGENTS.md`. Do not route through, install, launch, or
activate CStar, its kernel, Hall, Forge, AutoBot, or compatibility daemons.

Inspection, preservation, migration, and deterministic source validation are
allowed when explicitly in scope. Never read or print `.agents/config.json`.
Keep host-local state out of Git, preserve unrelated work, and treat passing
tests as source evidence rather than lifecycle or runtime acceptance.
